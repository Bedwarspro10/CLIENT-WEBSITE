Frequently asked

A few things
you may want
to know.

Every project is different. These answers offer a starting point; final scope and requirements are discussed with you.

What should I share before requesting a house plan?

Share the plot dimensions, location, road-facing side, any available site documents, number of floors, family requirements, preferred room count and approximate budget. A verified site survey may be needed before final drawings.

Can you design a house for my budget?

We can discuss your indicative budget and priorities at the start, then align the design brief accordingly. Actual construction costs depend on location, specifications, contractor rates and site conditions.

Do you provide 2D plans and 3D elevations?

These can be included as separate services or as part of a wider design package. The exact drawings, views, revisions and deliverables will be listed in the proposal.

Can you help with local building approval?

Approval requirements vary by local authority and project. The applicable rules, submission requirements and role of registered professionals should be confirmed for your specific site before work proceeds.

Do you take projects outside your city?

Initial consultations and parts of the design process may be coordinated remotely. Site visits, measurements, local consultants and execution support depend on project location and agreed scope.
